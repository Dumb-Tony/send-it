# Build 12 — Real Routes

14 September 2026. This pass answers the first campaign playtest: deliveries should feel like authored city routes rather than one long movement laboratory.

## Reconstruction

- The opening route puts collection beyond a jump gate. The next five require slide clearance, a conveyor launch, fan ascent, wall kicks, and a combined shutter/ramp line in sequence.
- Shift Two opens with an out-and-back job: cross town to collect the parcel, reverse direction, and deliver beside the original spawn. It then adds two chained conveyor gaps, a timed cargo-lift ascent, paired rooftop updrafts, a belt-to-awning collection followed by a fan finish, and the Skyline capstone.
- Eight deliveries place collection more than 400 world units from the spawn. Three put the parcel on an elevated platform. Collection can now be a meaningful first half of the route.
- The parcel grows from a tiny amber square into a high-contrast pink courier box with a dark border, cream tape, glow, animated locator beam and `COLLECT` callout.

## Verification

Route tests record actual movement evidence: sliding under shutters, conveyor contact and jumps, wall kicks, direction reversal, cargo-lift riding, and vertical fan travel. Each job still begins at its normal spawn, collects its authored parcel, reaches its destination, remains outside solids, and beats the initial Express target.
